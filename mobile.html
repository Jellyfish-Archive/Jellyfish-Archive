Welcome to the Jellyfish-Archive!

The Jellyfish Archive has been around since 2015 — built to preserve the best possible quality of every known video, performance, and recording for all fans to enjoy, forever.

We all miss Jellyfish, so the best thing we can do is relive the magic — by watching and listening to those legendary concerts, music videos, and interviews that made them so special.

Feel free to dive in and explore — you might discover something new or long forgotten. And if you’ve never heard of Jellyfish before... do yourself a favor and watch a few of their shows.

LET’S GO JELLYFISHIN’!

We are bringing classic Jellyfish videos into HD and 4K using the latest Starlight AI models. This high-quality processing is costly, so every contribution helps us restore and preserve more rare footage. Already restored videos are available in JellyTube.
